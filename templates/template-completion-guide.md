## 🚀 Ready to Use

All templates are complete and ready to use with your Urban Platter Flask application. Simply:

1. **Place templates in the `templates/` folder**
2. **Place CSS in `static/css/style.css`**
3. **Place JavaScript in `static/js/script.js`**
4. **Place logo in `static/images/urban-platter-logo.jpg`**
5. **Run your Flask application**
